Yomi-Snap Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Yomi-Snap helps you read Japanese and English on your screen: you capture the screen, tap a word, and see what it means. This policy explains what the app reads, what stays on your device, and what is sent elsewhere and why. It applies to Yomi-Snap on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and iOS.

The short version

  • Reading your screen happens on your device. Pictures of your screen are never uploaded.
  • Your history, saved words and any kept screenshots stay on your device.
  • Text leaves your device only when you ask for an AI translation (or ask AI to fill a saved word): the word and a little of the text around it go to our server and our AI providers.
  • There are no accounts. We do not sell your data and we show no ads.

What happens on your device

Screen capture. On Android, when you start the floating 読 button, Android asks your permission to capture the screen; you can share one app or the whole screen, and stop at any time from the status bar. Each time you tap 読, Yomi-Snap takes one still picture and recognises its text on the device. It records no video and no sound. On a computer, a capture is taken when you press the lookup shortcut.

Kept on your device: the words you look up and save, your lookup history, the text of each scan, and — while Save word images is on (Settings) — the picture of each scan, so a saved word can show where you read it. You can turn that setting off, delete saved words, or uninstall the app to remove all of it. Android backups and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this data.

Like any web server, ours sees the network (IP) address a request comes from. We do not store it: we keep only a one-way, salted fingerprint of it, used to limit how many requests one network can make. Our server runs on Supabase (servers in Singapore).

"Use what's playing" (Android, optional)

Off unless you turn it on in Settings › Translation. It needs Android's notification access for Yomi-Snap, which you give on Android's own settings page and can take back there at any time. Android warns that this access could let an app read your notifications; Yomi-Snap reads none. When you tap 読, it only asks Android which app is playing a video or song and the title that app shows (for example "Netflix" and the episode's name), and sends that with your AI translation as described above. It is not stored by us, and nothing is read when you are not tapping 読.

AI providers

AI answers come from Google (Gemini API) and, when Gemini cannot answer, OpenAI. They receive the text described above, but not your device identifier, network address or licence; OpenAI receives only a one-way fingerprint of the device so it can detect abuse.

  • Google Gemini. We currently use Gemini's free tier. Under Google's terms for it, Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and people at Google may read it. Please do not ask for AI translations of text that contains personal or sensitive information. Gemini API terms, Google Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models by default and keeps it for up to 30 days to detect abuse. OpenAI Privacy Policy.

What we keep, and for how long

  • For each AI request: the device identifier, the date, which provider and model answered, how many tokens it used and what it cost, and whether it succeeded. Not the text that was sent or the answer.
  • Each device's daily use count, a few usage events (such as a device reaching its daily limit), and short-lived rate-limit counters (deleted after two days).
  • Licences: a one-way hash of the key, its last four characters, the devices it is on, and for Google Play purchases, the purchase token and when the subscription ends.

We use this only to run the service: to count free uses, keep Pro working, stop abuse and keep costs in check. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or share it except with the providers named above.

Payments

Pro is sold through Google Play, which handles payment under Google's Privacy Policy. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play.

Your choices

  • Turn off AI translation in Settings, and nothing you read leaves your device.
  • Stop screen capture at any time from Android's status bar, or by closing the floating button.
  • To delete what our server keeps about your device, write to us with the Support ID shown in Settings › Licence (with advanced settings on); we will delete it.

Children

Yomi-Snap is meant for people aged 18 and over, and is not directed at children.

Security

Everything sent to our server travels over HTTPS. Device tokens and licence keys are stored only as hashes.

Changes

If we change what is read or sent, we will update this page and its date, and the app will ask again before capturing your screen.
